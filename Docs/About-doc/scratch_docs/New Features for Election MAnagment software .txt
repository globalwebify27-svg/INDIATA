Proposed App Enhancements – Complete Feature List

A. AI Features

1.AI Mode – Agentic AI

AI Mode will provide a ChatGPT-like interface within the application. 

The AI Agent will act as a digital party karyakarta/assistant, with a party-specific name and identity configured by the client. 

The bot's name, role, and introduction can be customized to match the respective party/organization. 

The AI Agent will understand natural-language and voice commands and perform supported application functions. 

Users can interact with the AI using their preferred/native language. 

AI Mode access will be controlled through the Client Panel based on the purchased AI subscription and permitted number of users.

2. AI Query Search

Users can search voter and application data using natural-language or voice queries instead of manually applying multiple filters.

AI will understand the query and automatically apply the required filter conditions.

Users can continue with follow-up queries within the same conversation.

AI can generate graphs based on the requested query/data.

3. AI Report Generation

Users can request reports directly through AI Mode using natural-language queries.

AI will apply the requested conditions and generate the required filtered report.

Reports can be generated in PDF, Excel, and CSV formats.

4. AI WhatsApp & SMS One-Way Campaign

AI can generate one-way WhatsApp and SMS campaign messages based on the campaign objective. 

Messages can be created in the selected/native language and tailored to the target audience. 

AI can generate messages for awareness, announcements, public outreach, campaign updates, and information sharing. 

Campaign managers can review and approve AI-generated messages before sending. 

Approved messages can be distributed to the selected audience through WhatsApp/SMS. 

The system can track messages sent, delivered, failed, campaign cost, and remaining credits. 

AI can analyze campaign performance and provide recommendations to improve future campaigns. 

5. AI Voice Calling – One-Way Campaign

AI can generate voice-call scripts and custom one-way campaign messages. 

The voice agent will communicate with users in the selected/native language. 

The system will deliver the pre-approved voice message to the targeted users as a one-way campaign. 

Users can receive the message but do not interact with the AI through the call. 

The system will track campaign results such as successful calls, rejected calls, failed calls, and unanswered calls. 

Calls can be recorded, where applicable, and campaign activity can be stored for tracking and reporting. 

The generated voice-call script requires human approval before the campaign is deployed. 

6. AI Campaign Status, Cost & Strategy Analysis

AI will provide a real-time overview of the overall campaign status from a single dashboard. 

The system will show how active and operational the campaign is, including ongoing, scheduled, completed, and inactive campaigns. 

AI will monitor campaign usage and performance across different communication channels. 

The dashboard will track SMS campaign usage, voice campaign usage, WhatsApp activity, and other outreach channels. 

The system will display campaign charges/costs incurred and provide a breakdown of spending by campaign and communication channel. 

AI will show the remaining SMS credits, voice campaign balance, WhatsApp usage/credits, and other available campaign resources. 

The system will identify low-balance or high-spending campaigns and alert the campaign team. 

AI will analyze campaign activity and recommend strategies to improve campaign performance and resource utilization. 

The system will highlight underperforming campaigns, inactive campaigns, high-performing campaigns, and areas requiring immediate attention. 

AI-generated recommendations will help campaign managers decide where to increase activity, reduce spending, or allocate additional resources.

7. Human-in-the-Loop AI

Read operations such as AI search, filtering, graph generation, data analysis, summaries, and report generation can run without manual approval.

Write/action operations such as WhatsApp scripts, chatbot actions, voice-call scripts, and AI actions that modify or communicate data require human approval before execution.

8. Native-Language AI

AI will accept queries and provide responses in the selected/native language.

Native-language support will apply to AI Mode, search, WhatsApp chatbot, voice calling, surveys, feedback processing, and summaries.

B. Non-AI Features

9. Geo-Tagged Survey

The system will capture the survey member's live location while collecting survey data.

Latitude and longitude coordinates will be stored with the survey record.

Location information will be maintained for doorstep survey activities.

10. Interactive Dashboard Graphs & Analytics

The Client Mobile App will display key statistics using horizontal bar graphs.

The Client Website will display statistics using pie charts.

Dashboards will show loaded/completed data percentage, gender-wise count, caste-wise count, most populated booths, and most-reported public demands.

11. BLO Officer Information - window/Panel

A separate page will contain Booth Level Officer (BLO) information.

BLO details will be organized booth-wise.

The page will contain the BLO's name and contact details.

Users will be able to search/access BLO information according to the booth.

12. Campaign Management, Tracking & Script Management - window/Panel

Tracking: A dedicated section will display the overall status and activity of WhatsApp, Campaign SMS, and Voice campaigns, including active, completed, paused, and ongoing campaigns. 

Campaign Usage: Track the number of WhatsApp messages sent, SMS sent, and voice calls made, along with successful, rejected, and failed calls. 

Campaign Balance: Display the remaining campaign balance separately for WhatsApp messages, SMS, and voice calls, including the available number of messages/calls remaining. 

Recharge Management: Provide recharge options for all three campaign types, with the available recharge plans and their corresponding message/call limits and remaining balance. 

Script Management: Provide a separate section to view and manage WhatsApp scripts, SMS scripts, and Voice Call scripts. 

Script Editing: Allow authorized users to view, edit, and update campaign scripts before they are used in campaigns. 

Campaign Overview: Provide a consolidated view of campaign status, message/call usage, successful and rejected calls, remaining campaign balance, recharge options, and available campaign scripts.

13. Previous Election Analysis & Strategy Window/Panel

Historical Election Data: A dedicated section will provide access to previous election data, including party-wise and candidate-wise results, voting patterns, and available historical election information. 

Locality & Booth Analysis: Users will be able to view previous election results for a specific constituency, locality, booth, or area, including the number of votes received by each party/candidate. 

Candidate-Specific Analysis: The candidate's information collected during profile creation/login will be used to present relevant historical results and analysis for their selected constituency/locality. 

Previous Election Strategies: The section will display available information about strategies, campaign approaches, and election performance of different parties/candidates from previous elections. 

Booth-Wise Results: Users can view previous election results booth-wise, including which party/candidate received how many votes and the respective voting distribution. 

Comparative Analysis: Historical results can be viewed to understand party-wise and candidate-wise performance across different booths/localities. 

RAG-Based AI Bot: A dedicated RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot will be available within this section. It will answer questions using the stored historical election data and documents. 

14. Profile & Party Settings- window/Panel

Profile Window: A dedicated profile window will open when the user clicks the Profile icon in the application. 

Account Details: Display the user's account information and relevant profile details. 

Party Details: Display the associated party/organization information, including party name and other configured details. 

Party Branding: Provide options to manage party-related logos, images, banners, and other branding elements. 

Page Theme: Allow the client to configure the application's theme and visual appearance according to the party/organization branding. 

Profile & Configuration: Provide a centralized place to view and manage the account, party information, images, and page-level branding settings. 

Note: Subscription-Based AI Access – AI Mode Access Control

AI Mode will be available as an option within the application for all users; however, actual access will be restricted to authorized users. The Client Panel that purchases the AI subscription will have an AI Mode Access Management option within the User Panel, allowing the client to select specific members who are permitted to use AI Mode. The number of users who can be authorized will depend on the purchased subscription plan. The client can grant, revoke, or change AI Mode access for selected members at any time, provided the number of authorized users remains within the subscription limit.

Example:

If the client purchases a plan allowing 3 AI users:

Client Panel

     ↓

User Management

     ↓

AI Mode Access

     ↓

☑ Member A

☑ Member B

☑ Member C

☐ Member D

☐ Member E

Only Member A, B, and C will be able to use AI Mode. If the client later wants Member D to use AI Mode, access can be revoked from one existing member and assigned to Member D, while remaining within the 3-user subscription limit.
